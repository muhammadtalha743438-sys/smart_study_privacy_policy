Privacy Policy for Smart Study

Application: Smart Study
Package Name: com.aistudio.smartstudy.aiqpmz
Effective Date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Smart Study ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our Android mobile application. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding our data practices.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Smart Study is designed as a privacy-conscious study companion. We collect and store minimal data necessary to provide intelligent tutoring features:

  • User Study Queries and Chat History: The questions, study prompts, subject preferences, and chat messages you enter into the application.
  • Saved Notes and Bookmarks: Key concepts, study cards, and formula summaries that you explicitly choose to bookmark or save within the app.
  • Optional API Configuration: If you provide a custom Google Gemini API key or select preferred AI models, this configuration is stored locally on your device.

2. Data Storage and Local Persistence

On-Device Local Storage: All your conversation history, study sessions, and notes are stored strictly locally on your device using an encrypted/sandboxed SQLite Room database. We do not operate a proprietary remote server or database to harvest your private study records.

Your local study history never leaves your device unless a query is sent to the AI service for processing.

3. Third-Party AI Services (Google Gemini API)

To provide real-time study tutoring, explanations, and quiz generation, Smart Study transmits your study queries and relevant preceding conversation context to Google's Gemini API (Google Generative AI).

  • Data transmitted includes your current prompt, subject context, and relevant recent messages within that study thread to ensure contextual continuity.
  • This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the Google APIs Terms of Service.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your chat data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Device Permissions

Smart Study requires only the following standard permission:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required exclusively to communicate with the Google Gemini API endpoint to receive AI-generated study responses. We do not access location, contacts, camera, or personal media files.

5. User Data Control and Deletion

You have full autonomy over your data at all times:

  • Delete Individual Sessions: You can delete any individual chat thread from the navigation menu at any time.
  • Delete Saved Notes: You can review and delete any saved note or flashcard from the Study Notes screen.
  • Clear All App Data: You can clear all stored messages, sessions, and settings using the "Clear All Data" button in the in-app Privacy Policy screen or via Android System Settings > Apps > Smart Study > Storage > Clear Storage.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Smart Study provides educational study assistance. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information (such as names, addresses, phone numbers, or social security numbers) from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in the European Economic Area). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has submitted personal details, please contact us so we can guide you in removing local data.

7. Security

We value your trust. All network transmissions to the Gemini API occur over secure, encrypted HTTPS protocols (TLS 1.2/1.3). On-device data is protected by the Android sandbox architecture preventing other unauthorized apps from accessing your local database.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated "Effective Date" and updated within the in-app Privacy Policy screen.
